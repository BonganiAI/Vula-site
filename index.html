Company overview

The OES of the VULA range

VULA Drilling (Pty) Ltd is the Original Equipment Supplier of our “VULA” range of rock drilling consumables. Our range covers hard-rock drilling consumables for top-hammer and DTH equipment — taper and threaded drill bits, drill rods, shanks, couplings, adaptors and specialised accessories.

From our head office in Apex Industrial, Benoni, with easy access to major transport routes, we respond quickly to customer needs across the country.

Our story

A local alternative, built for South African mines.

VULA was founded in 2005 to develop a local company supplying a new range of drilling products for the South African mining industry — an alternative to the multinationals that dominated the market at the time.

  1. 2005

    Founded, with an early focus on supplying tapered drill bits.

  2. Growth

    Expanded to cover a vast range of the drilling consumables the industry needs.

  3. 2025

    ISO 9001:2015 certified for the source and supply of top-hammer and DTH drilling consumables.

Good to know

Frequently asked

What products do you supply? +

Hard-rock drilling consumables for top-hammer and DTH equipment: tapered and threaded drill bits, tapered and threaded drill rods, 22 & 25 Hex taper drill steel, drifter and extension rods, shanks, couplings, adaptors and other specialised accessories.

Do you supply conventional and mechanised mining? +

Yes. Our conventional range covers tapered bits, tapered rods and knock-off tools; our mechanised range covers threaded bits, threaded rods, couplings and adaptors.

Are you ISO certified? +

Yes. VULA Drilling is certified to ISO 9001:2015 by CertX SA for the source and supply of top-hammer and down-the-hole (DTH) drilling consumables.

Where are you based? +

Our head office is at 2 London Street, Apex Industrial, Benoni, in the Ekurhuleni Metro. The central location and easy access to major transport routes mean we can respond quickly to customer needs.

Do you support local manufacturing? +

As a 100% South African owned company, we support local manufacturing through a network of highly specialised manufacturing partners, and work to develop small suppliers in communities close to mining areas.
